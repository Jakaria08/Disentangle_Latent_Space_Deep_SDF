\section{Setting}

\subsection{Frozen latent representation}

Longitudinal left-hippocampus surfaces are compressed once, offline, into a fixed
$D = 128$ dimensional latent space by a \emph{frozen} representation. Three
interchangeable representations are used, all producing the same latent dimension:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Name & Decoder $\Dec$ & Validation coordinate RMSE \\
\midrule
\texttt{pca128}       & first 128 components of a train-only PCA-150 basis (linear) & --- \\
\texttt{spiralnet128} & saved SpiralNet autoencoder decoder & $0.03721$ mm \\
\texttt{adaptive128}  & saved Adaptive-Spiral autoencoder decoder & $0.03676$ mm \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Latents are standardized with train-only moments, so a state is
$\z \in \R^{D}$ with $\z = (\z_{\mathrm{raw}} - \mu_z)/\sigma_z$. The decoder
\begin{equation}
  \Dec : \R^{D} \longrightarrow \R^{N \times 3}, \qquad N = 2746,
\end{equation}
maps a standardized latent to millimetre vertex coordinates. For \texttt{pca128} this is
the affine map $\Dec(\z) = \bigl((\z \odot \sigma_z + \mu_z) W_{\mathrm{pca}} + \bar{\bm{x}}\bigr)$
reshaped to $N \times 3$; for the two autoencoders it is the saved decoder network.

\paragraph{Decoder contract.} $\Dec$ has \texttt{requires\_grad=False} and stays in
evaluation mode, but it \emph{remains inside the differentiable graph}. Reconstruction
gradients therefore flow through $\Dec$ into the predicted latent and into the flow, while
no decoder weight is ever updated. The trainer explicitly audits this at startup: it
backpropagates the vertex reconstruction term alone and asserts that the flow receives a
strictly positive gradient norm while every decoder parameter gradient is \texttt{None}.

\paragraph{Mesh volume.} For vertices $\X$ with fixed face set $\mathcal{F}$,
\begin{equation}
  \vol(\X) = \Biggl| \frac{1}{6} \sum_{(a,b,c) \in \mathcal{F}}
    \bigl\langle \X_a,\; \X_b \times \X_c \bigr\rangle \Biggr| .
\end{equation}

\subsection{Data}

A longitudinal observation is $(\z_s, \z_t, s, t, d)$: two visits of the same subject at
ages $s, t$ (years) with diagnosis $d \in \{0,1\}$, $0 = \mathrm{CN}$, $1 = \mathrm{AD}$.
Write $\Delta = t - s$. Some pairs carry an observed intermediate visit at age $a_m$.
Subjects are additionally available as whole trajectories
$(\z_0, \dots, \z_{n-1})$ at ages $(a_0, \dots, a_{n-1})$.

\subsection{Train-only normalization scales}

Every loss term is divided by a robust scale estimated on the training split only, so the
thirteen terms are commensurate at initialization. With $\Delta\z = \z_t - \z_s$ and
$\Delta\X = \Dec(\z_t) - \Dec(\z_s)$, each scale is the median over subject-balanced
training pairs:
\begin{align}
  \sigma_{\mathrm{lat}} &= \operatorname{med}\bigl[ \tfrac{1}{D}\|\Delta\z\|_2^2 \bigr], &
  \sigma_{\mathrm{crd}} &= \operatorname{med}\bigl[ \tfrac{1}{3N}\|\Delta\X\|_1 \bigr], &
  \sigma_{\mathrm{euc}} &= \operatorname{med}\bigl[ \tfrac{1}{N}\textstyle\sum_i \|\Delta\X_i\|_2 \bigr], \nonumber\\
  \sigma_{\mathrm{vol}} &= \operatorname{med}\bigl[ |\Delta \log \vol| \bigr], &
  \sigma_{\mathrm{rate}} &= \operatorname{med}\bigl[ |\Delta \log \vol / \Delta y| \bigr], &
  \sigma_{\mathrm{dsp}} &= \operatorname{med}\bigl[ \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{D}}\|\Delta\z\|_2 \bigr],
\end{align}
plus $\sigma_{\mathrm{slp}}$, the median over training subjects of the absolute
least-squares slope of $\log \vol$ against years. Group targets
$\rho_{\mathrm{CN}}, \rho_{\mathrm{AD}}$ are subject-averaged log-volume rates per
diagnosis, and $\rho_{\Delta} = \rho_{\mathrm{AD}} - \rho_{\mathrm{CN}}$.

\section{The transport map}

\subsection{Definition}

The flow is \textbf{not} an ODE. There is no integrator, no adjoint, and no attention. A
single network emits an average latent velocity over the interval $[s,t]$, and transport is
one explicit residual step:
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation}
  \Phi_\theta(\z, s, t, d) \;=\; \z \;+\; (t - s)\,
  \bigl[ \vel^{\mathrm{CN}}_\theta(\z, s, t) + d\,\vel^{\mathrm{AD}}_\theta(\z, s, t) \bigr].
  \label{eq:transport}
\end{empheq}
The instantaneous velocity is the diagonal $\vel_\theta(\z,a,d) = \Phi$'s time derivative at
$t = a$, obtained by evaluating the same network at $s = t = a$.

\subsection{Structural properties}

\paragraph{Exact identity.} For all $\z, a, d, \theta$:
$\Phi_\theta(\z, a, a, d) = \z$ bit-exactly, because the elapsed-time factor $(t-s)$
multiplies the network output. No penalty enforces this and no integrator can erode it.

\paragraph{Exact no-change initialization.} Both output heads are zero-initialized, so
$\Phi_{\theta_0} = \mathrm{id}$. Epoch~0 is exactly the no-change baseline and is saved as
the initial \texttt{best.pt}.

\paragraph{Cocycle / semigroup property (learned).} The target algebraic law is
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation}
  \Phi_\theta\bigl(\Phi_\theta(\z, s, r, d),\, r,\, t,\, d\bigr) = \Phi_\theta(\z, s, t, d)
  \qquad \forall\, r,
  \label{eq:cocycle}
\end{empheq}
whose $r = t$ instance is the inverse law
$\Phi_\theta(\Phi_\theta(\z,s,t,d),t,s,d) = \z$. Unlike the identity, \eqref{eq:cocycle}
is \emph{not} structural: because $\vel_\theta$ depends on the endpoints $(s,t)$ and on the
state $\z$, composing two sub-intervals invokes the network at different arguments than the
direct interval. Agreement is imposed by penalty and audited as a validation gate. The
coboundary term of the original C4 formulation is prohibited by config validation and fixed
to exactly zero.

\section{Network}

\texttt{DirectC4Flow} is a small conditioned residual MLP. With width $W = 256$ and
$B = 2$ residual blocks, the input feature vector is the latent concatenated with five
scalar time features:
\begin{equation}
  \bm{f} = \Bigl[\, \z \;\Vert\; s \;\Vert\; t \;\Vert\; (t-s) \;\Vert\; |t-s| \;\Vert\;
  \tfrac{1}{2}(s+t) \,\Bigr] \in \R^{D+5} = \R^{133}.
\end{equation}
Note that both endpoints enter, so the field is a function of the interval and not merely of
its length. The trunk is
\begin{align}
  \bm{h}_0 &= \operatorname{SiLU}\bigl(W_{\mathrm{in}} \bm{f} + \bm{b}_{\mathrm{in}}\bigr), \\
  \bm{h}_b &= \operatorname{SiLU}\Bigl( \bm{h}_{b-1} +
    W_2 \operatorname{SiLU}\bigl(W_1 \operatorname{LN}(\bm{h}_{b-1})\bigr) \Bigr),
    \qquad b = 1, \dots, B,
  \label{eq:resblock}
\end{align}
and the two zero-initialized heads give the gated velocity
\begin{equation}
  \vel_\theta(\z,s,t,d) = W_{\mathrm{CN}} \bm{h}_B + \bm{b}_{\mathrm{CN}}
  \;+\; d\,\bigl( W_{\mathrm{AD}} \bm{h}_B + \bm{b}_{\mathrm{AD}} \bigr).
\end{equation}
The diagnosis label enters \emph{only} through this output gate, never as a trunk input.

\paragraph{Parameters.} $364{,}288$ total: input $34{,}304$; two residual blocks
$264{,}192$; CN head $32{,}896$; AD-residual head $32{,}896$.

\paragraph{Remark on \eqref{eq:resblock}.} The activation is applied to the sum,
$\bm{h}_b = \operatorname{SiLU}(\bm{h}_{b-1} + f(\bm{h}_{b-1}))$, rather than to the branch
only. This is a post-activation residual and does not preserve a clean identity path
through the trunk.

\section{Objective}

\subsection{Reduction helpers}

For a predicted latent $\hat{\z}$ against target latent $\z$ with reference vertices $\X$:
\begin{align}
  \ell_{\mathrm{lat}}(\hat{\z}, \z) &= \frac{1}{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\,
    \bigl\langle (\hat{\z} - \z)^2 \bigr\rangle, \\
  \ell_{\mathrm{crd}}(\hat{\z}, \X) &= \frac{1}{\sigma_{\mathrm{crd}}}\,
    \bigl\langle \bigl| \Dec(\hat{\z}) - \X \bigr| \bigr\rangle, \\
  \ell_{\mathrm{euc}}(\hat{\z}, \X) &= \frac{1}{\sigma_{\mathrm{euc}}}\,
    \Bigl\langle \bigl\| \Dec(\hat{\z})_i - \X_i \bigr\|_2 \Bigr\rangle_i, \\
  \ell_{\mathrm{vtx}}(\hat{\z}, \X) &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl[
    \ell_{\mathrm{crd}}(\hat{\z}, \X) + \ell_{\mathrm{euc}}(\hat{\z}, \X) \bigr],
    \label{eq:vtx}\\
  m_\sigma(\bm{a}, \bm{b}) &= \frac{1}{\sigma}\bigl\langle (\bm{a} - \bm{b})^2 \bigr\rangle,
\end{align}
where $\langle\cdot\rangle$ denotes the mean over all remaining axes. Equation
\eqref{eq:vtx} deliberately averages a per-coordinate $L_1$ term with a true per-vertex
Euclidean distance, so the loss is not dominated by axis-aligned error.

\subsection{Pair terms}

With $\hat{\z}_t = \Phi_\theta(\z_s, s, t, d)$ and $\hat{\z}_s = \Phi_\theta(\z_t, t, s, d)$:

\paragraph{1--2. Real reconstruction (latent and decoded vertex).}
\begin{align}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{r\text{-}lat}} &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl[
    \ell_{\mathrm{lat}}(\hat{\z}_t, \z_t) + \ell_{\mathrm{lat}}(\hat{\z}_s, \z_s) \bigr], \\
  \Loss_{\mathrm{r\text{-}vtx}} &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl[
    \ell_{\mathrm{vtx}}(\hat{\z}_t, \X_t) + \ell_{\mathrm{vtx}}(\hat{\z}_s, \X_s) \bigr].
\end{align}

\paragraph{3. Observed semigroup.} For pairs possessing a real intermediate visit at $a_m$,
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{obs}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl[
    m_{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\bigl(\hat{\z}_t, \Phi(\Phi(\z_s,s,a_m),a_m,t)\bigr)
  + m_{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\bigl(\hat{\z}_s, \Phi(\Phi(\z_t,t,a_m),a_m,s)\bigr) \Bigr].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{4. Virtual semigroup.} Draw $u \sim \mathcal{U}(0.2, 0.8)$, set $a_v = s + u\Delta$,
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{vrt}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl[
    m_{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\bigl(\hat{\z}_t, \Phi(\Phi(\z_s,s,a_v),a_v,t)\bigr)
  + m_{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\bigl(\hat{\z}_s, \Phi(\Phi(\z_t,t,a_v),a_v,s)\bigr) \Bigr].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{5. Inverse consistency.}
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{inv}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl[
    m_{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\bigl(\Phi(\hat{\z}_t, t, s), \z_s\bigr)
  + m_{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\bigl(\Phi(\hat{\z}_s, s, t), \z_t\bigr) \Bigr].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{6. Decoded volume.} With $\hat{V}_t = \vol(\Dec(\hat{\z}_t))$ and
$\hat{V}_s = \vol(\Dec(\hat{\z}_s))$,
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{vol}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl[
    \SL\Bigl(\tfrac{\log \hat{V}_t - \log V_t}{\sigma_{\mathrm{vol}}}, 0\Bigr) +
    \SL\Bigl(\tfrac{\log \hat{V}_s - \log V_s}{\sigma_{\mathrm{vol}}}, 0\Bigr) \Bigr].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{7. Log-volume rate.} With $y = \max(\Delta, 10^{-6})$,
\begin{equation}
  r_{\mathrm{obs}} = \frac{\log V_t - \log V_s}{y}, \quad
  r_f = \frac{\log \hat{V}_t - \log V_s}{y}, \quad
  r_b = \frac{\log \hat{V}_s - \log V_t}{-y},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{rate}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl[
    \SL\Bigl(\tfrac{r_f - r_{\mathrm{obs}}}{\sigma_{\mathrm{rate}}}, 0\Bigr) +
    \SL\Bigl(\tfrac{r_b - r_{\mathrm{obs}}}{\sigma_{\mathrm{rate}}}, 0\Bigr) \Bigr].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{8--9. Diagnosis-group rate and AD$-$CN gap.} Active only when both groups occur
in the batch:
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{grp}} = \frac{1}{2}\!\!\sum_{g \in \{\mathrm{CN},\mathrm{AD}\}}\!\!
    \SL\Bigl(\tfrac{\overline{r}_f^{\,g} - \rho_g}{\sigma_{\mathrm{rate}}}, 0\Bigr),
  \qquad
  \Loss_{\mathrm{gap}} = \SL\Bigl(
    \tfrac{(\overline{r}_f^{\,\mathrm{AD}} - \overline{r}_f^{\,\mathrm{CN}}) - \rho_\Delta}
          {\sigma_{\mathrm{rate}}}, 0\Bigr).
\end{equation}

\subsection{Sequence terms}

Exactly one full subject trajectory $(\z_0,\dots,\z_{n-1})$ at ages $(a_0,\dots,a_{n-1})$ is
evaluated per optimizer step, alternating CN and AD. Four predicted families are formed:
\begin{alignat}{2}
  \text{direct forward:}\quad  &\hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{df}}_k = \Phi(\z_0, a_0, a_k, d),
  &\qquad k &= 1,\dots,n-1, \\
  \text{rollout forward:}\quad &\hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{rf}}_k = \Phi(\hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{rf}}_{k-1}, a_{k-1}, a_k, d),
  &\qquad \hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{rf}}_0 &= \z_0, \\
  \text{direct backward:}\quad &\hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{db}}_k = \Phi(\z_{n-1}, a_{n-1}, a_k, d),
  &\qquad k &= n-2,\dots,0, \\
  \text{rollout backward:}\quad &\hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{rb}}_k = \Phi(\hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{rb}}_{k+1}, a_{k+1}, a_k, d).&&
\end{alignat}

\paragraph{10--11. Sequence reconstruction.} Averaging the four families,
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{s\text{-}lat}} = \tfrac{1}{4}\!\!\sum_{\star \in \{\mathrm{df,rf,db,rb}\}}\!\!
    \ell_{\mathrm{lat}}(\hat{\z}^{\,\star}, \z^{\star}), \qquad
  \Loss_{\mathrm{s\text{-}vtx}} = \tfrac{1}{4}\!\!\sum_{\star}\!
    \ell_{\mathrm{vtx}}(\hat{\z}^{\,\star}, \X^{\star}).
\end{equation}

\paragraph{12. Sequence semigroup.} The direct and rolled-out families must agree; this is
\eqref{eq:cocycle} applied along a whole observed trajectory:
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{s\text{-}sg}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl[
    m_{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\bigl(\hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{df}}, \hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{rf}}\bigr) +
    m_{\sigma_{\mathrm{lat}}}\bigl(\hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{db}}, \hat{\z}^{\,\mathrm{rb}}\bigr) \Bigr].
\end{equation}

\paragraph{13. Volume-trajectory slope.} With
$\operatorname{slp}(\bm{y}, \bm{v}) = \sum_k (y_k - \bar{y})(v_k - \bar{v}) / \sum_k (y_k-\bar{y})^2$
the least-squares slope, and $\hat{V}^{\mathrm{f}}, \hat{V}^{\mathrm{b}}$ the decoded volume
trajectories obtained by prepending the true anchor visit,
\begin{equation}
  \Loss_{\mathrm{slp}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\Bigl[
    \SL\Bigl(\tfrac{\operatorname{slp}(\bm{y}, \log \hat{V}^{\mathrm{f}}) - \operatorname{slp}(\bm{y}, \log V)}{\sigma_{\mathrm{slp}}}, 0\Bigr)
  + \SL\Bigl(\tfrac{\operatorname{slp}(\bm{y}^{\mathrm{rev}}, \log \hat{V}^{\mathrm{b}}) - \operatorname{slp}(\bm{y}, \log V)}{\sigma_{\mathrm{slp}}}, 0\Bigr) \Bigr].
\end{equation}

\subsection{Curriculum ramps and total loss}

With $E_c = 10$ and $E_a = 15$, at epoch $e$:
\begin{equation}
  \rho_c(e) = \min\!\left(1, \frac{e}{E_c}\right), \qquad
  \rho_a(e) = \min\!\left(1, \frac{e}{E_a}\right).
\end{equation}
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{align}
  \Loss(e) =\;\;& w_{\mathrm{r\text{-}lat}}\Loss_{\mathrm{r\text{-}lat}}
                 + w_{\mathrm{r\text{-}vtx}}\Loss_{\mathrm{r\text{-}vtx}} \nonumber\\
  &+ \rho_c(e)\Bigl[ w_{\mathrm{obs}}\Loss_{\mathrm{obs}} + w_{\mathrm{vrt}}\Loss_{\mathrm{vrt}}
     + w_{\mathrm{inv}}\Loss_{\mathrm{inv}}
     + w_{\mathrm{s\text{-}lat}}\Loss_{\mathrm{s\text{-}lat}}
     + w_{\mathrm{s\text{-}vtx}}\Loss_{\mathrm{s\text{-}vtx}}
     + w_{\mathrm{s\text{-}sg}}\Loss_{\mathrm{s\text{-}sg}} \Bigr] \nonumber\\
  &+ \rho_a(e)\Bigl[ w_{\mathrm{vol}}\Loss_{\mathrm{vol}} + w_{\mathrm{rate}}\Loss_{\mathrm{rate}}
     + w_{\mathrm{slp}}\Loss_{\mathrm{slp}} + w_{\mathrm{grp}}\Loss_{\mathrm{grp}}
     + w_{\mathrm{gap}}\Loss_{\mathrm{gap}} \Bigr].
  \label{eq:total}
\end{empheq}

\section{Training procedure}

\subsection{Stratified sampling}

Pairs are drawn by a \texttt{WeightedRandomSampler} with replacement. Weight of a pair
belonging to subject $u$ in stratum $\kappa \in \{\mathrm{CN},\mathrm{AD}\} \times
\{\text{adjacent}, \text{non-adjacent}\}$:
\begin{equation}
  w(\text{pair}) = \frac{0.25}{|\mathcal{U}_\kappa| \cdot |\mathcal{P}_{u,\kappa}|},
\end{equation}
giving each of the four strata equal mass, each subject within a stratum equal mass, and
each pair within a subject equal mass. Sequence subjects are drawn from a separate
CN/AD-alternating shuffle, one trajectory per optimizer step.

denotes a $12\times$ different amount of optimization across the three runs}.

\subsection{Validation and model selection}

For group $g$ on first-to-last subject pairs, let $c_g, e_g, \ell_g$ be the ratios of
predicted to no-change coordinate error, Euclidean error, and latent error, and let
$\bar{c}, \bar{e}, \bar{\nu}$ be the corresponding all-pair coordinate, Euclidean, and
relative-volume ratios. The selection score is
\begin{empheq}[box=\fbox]{equation}
  S = \underbrace{\frac{1}{2}\!\!\sum_{g \in \{\mathrm{CN},\mathrm{AD}\}}\!\! \frac{c_g + e_g}{2}}
      _{\text{macro first-to-last shape}}
    \;+\; \underbrace{0.2 \cdot \frac{\bar{c} + \bar{e}}{2}}_{\text{all-pair shape}}
    \;+\; \underbrace{0.001 \cdot \bar{\nu}}_{\text{volume tie-break}} .
  \label{eq:score}
\end{empheq}
A checkpoint is eligible only if all of the following hold:
\begin{equation}
  \ell_g \le 1.01 \;\;\text{and}\;\; c_g \le 1.01 \;\; \forall g \in \{\mathrm{CN}, \mathrm{AD}\},
  \qquad
  \bar{D}_{\mathrm{sg}} \le 0.25, \qquad
  \bar{D}_{\mathrm{inv}} \le 0.25, \qquad S \text{ finite},
  \label{eq:gates}
\end{equation}
where the relative midpoint defects, normalized by the displacement scale
$\sigma_{\mathrm{dsp}}$, are
\begin{equation}
  D_{\mathrm{sg}} = \frac{\bigl\| \Phi(\z_s,s,t) - \Phi(\Phi(\z_s,s,\tfrac{s+t}{2}),\tfrac{s+t}{2},t) \bigr\|_2 / \sqrt{D}}{\sigma_{\mathrm{dsp}}},
  \qquad
  D_{\mathrm{inv}} = \frac{\bigl\| \Phi(\Phi(\z_s,s,t),t,s) - \z_s \bigr\|_2 / \sqrt{D}}{\sigma_{\mathrm{dsp}}}.
\end{equation}
The best checkpoint updates only when feasible and $S < S_{\mathrm{best}} - 10^{-5}$;
training halts after 30 consecutive epochs without such an update. The epoch-0 identity map
seeds $S_{\mathrm{best}}$, and for the identity all ratios equal 1, so
$S_{\mathrm{best}}^{(0)} = 1 + 0.2 + 0.001 = 1.201$.

$1.021 \to 0.790$ and $\Loss_{\mathrm{s\text{-}vtx}}$ falls $1.046 \to 0.736$ over 31
epochs. The reported total \eqref{eq:total} nonetheless \emph{rises} during epochs 1--15
purely because $\rho_c$ and $\rho_a$ are still opening; it is not comparable across the ramp
window.

Second, validation degrades from the very first epoch. The macro first-to-last shape ratio
for \texttt{spiralnet128} moves $0.899 \to 1.102$, i.e. from $10\%$ better than no-change to
$10\%$ worse, while the semigroup and inverse defects grow from $\approx 0$ (trivially
satisfied at the identity initialization) to $0.056$ and $0.187$. Feasibility under
\eqref{eq:gates} is lost at epoch 4 through the per-group $c_g \le 1.01$ condition, long
before the loose $0.25$ defect gates bind.

Notably, the \emph{volumetric} predictions are accurate and remain stable: at epoch 1 the
predicted first-to-last log-volume rates are $-0.0129$ (CN) and $-0.0440$ (AD) against
observed $-0.0142$ and $-0.0445$, and the volume ratio stays near $0.45$-$0.49$ for the
whole run. The model captures the group-level atrophy trend and holds it; what degrades is
the per-vertex spatial direction of the predicted latent displacement.
