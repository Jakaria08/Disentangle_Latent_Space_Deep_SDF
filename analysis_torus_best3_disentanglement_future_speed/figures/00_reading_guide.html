Reading guide: what each plot means

Definitions for interpreting the saved plots and final model choice.

This notebook separates three questions that are often mixed together:

1. Velocity disentanglement

A good disentangled model puts disease-related shape change in the disease component, ordinary aging in the age component, and little coherent progression in residual.

2. Test-time disease prediction

Only the 64/160/32 model has a baseline-anchor disease head. The old 64/128/64 and additive models use an oracle disease condition in these visualizations.

3. Future generation

Forecast quality must be judged separately from velocity disentanglement. A model may reconstruct future scans well while using a poorly separated velocity decomposition.

Important: the current future volume-error numbers have a mesh-scale mismatch. Use SDF MAE and visual shape comparison first; do not rank models by the current volume error.
