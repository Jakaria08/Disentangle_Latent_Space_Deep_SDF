Important failure to fix next

The 64/160/32 model is promising but needs better disease-head calibration and validation. Its residual branch is clean, but false-positive disease prediction creates false disease speed on a healthy subject. The next experiment should keep the 64/160/32 block design and improve disease prediction/calibration rather than returning to the residual-heavy 64/128/64 design.

How to read the surface colors

Surface colors are not diagnosis probability. They are signed anatomical motion induced by a velocity component through the decoder Jacobian. Red/blue indicate opposite normal directions. INR-difference maps show finite field change; Jacobian maps show decoder sensitivity.
